## Outreach order

1. Approach specialist implementation partners first. They may have an existing client need and a route to multiple teams.
2. Approach mid-sized product engineering teams with a specific one-repository evaluation.
3. Treat large enterprise accounts as longer-cycle strategic prospects. Do not assume a public case study implies buying intent.

## Message angle

Lead with an engineering control problem, not generic AI safety:
- Human-authored session scope.
- Tool-call enforcement.
- Git-backed verification of changed files.
- Acceptance-check evidence.
- A portable receipt for review.

Ask for one repository and one representative workflow. Avoid asking for a broad platform procurement meeting.

## Research and contact rules

- Re-check the source and current role before outreach.
- Use official company contact pages, public business email addresses or professional networking routes.
- Do not guess email addresses or scrape personal contact information.
- Personalise each message to a verified role and documented workflow.
- Keep a record of opt-outs and do not follow up after a clear refusal.
